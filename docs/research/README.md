# Research and historical technical evidence

These standalone HTML/SVG/JavaScript studies and technical notes are research, not production Quickshell features. Their accepted or rejected status is important. Historical prototypes preserve their original bytes; public technical notes may use updated provenance and status language.

## Reading order

- [Product specifications](../product/) and [design decisions](../design-decisions.md) establish the current intended behavior.
- [Current state](../development/CURRENT_STATE.md), source and tests establish implementation and verification status.
- [November 20–23 B6 studies and notes](prototypes/2026-11-20/README.md) preserve the browser geometry and interaction chronology. B6r12b3 is the accepted scroll interaction reference, not a production backend.
- [November 24 B7 studies](prototypes/2026-11-24/README.md) identify B7r3 artwork, B7s11 sculpture, B7u3 upright and B7p10 parcel references. See the [representation asset contract](../product/REPRESENTATION_ASSETS.md) for pinned source hashes and reproducible extraction. B7p3 was rejected; the failed byte-identical initial P9 delivery is not an accepted reference.
- [B8 workshop and S20 feature matrix](prototypes/2026-11-24/b8/S20_REFERENCE_MATRIX.md) — feature-specific design sources, supersession and deferred research; no native acceptance implied.
- [November 17 standalone prototypes](handoffs/2026-11-17/prototypes/) preserve early book, Map Room, Storage Workshop and Gallery studies. [Storage Workshop v5.4](handoffs/2026-11-17/prototypes/Astral_Archive_Storage_Workshop_v5_4.html) is historical provenance; later B6/B7 work supersedes its implementation role.

The [B6 chronology](../development/B6_RESEARCH_STATUS_2026-11-23.md), [browser/native comparison](../development/B6_NATIVE_BROWSER_COMPARISON_2026-11-24.md), [B7 native work](../development/B7_NATIVE_REPRESENTATION_WORK.md) and [mixed cubby checkpoint](../development/B7_N2_MIXED_CUBBIES.md) record named historical checks, failures and limitations. Only reviewed research inputs are distributed here. Unpublished reference images, embedded font specimens and private continuity records are not dependencies of the technical project. Do not reconstruct missing source or invent reference photographs. The public product specifications and source are the starting point for new contributors; older prototypes cannot override them.
