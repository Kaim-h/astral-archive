02 · The scroll rack

One continuous partitioned rack. Lines and their intersections define each square, diamond, or triangular cell; hexes form a separate tessellation.

Calculating geometry…

Click any cell to open its enlarged rummaging view. Each cell has its own saved demonstration placements, with individual drops or gap insertion.

Selected cellCalculating…

Rummage: click a scroll for metadata. In Arrange, drag it to any collision-free position within the same cell.

Gap insertion: faint rings mark candidate vacancies. Hover a free pocket to see the proposed scroll; then choose Insert at gap.

Top drop: the new scroll enters near the upper edge and settles only along a collision-free route. If the entrance is blocked, insert it instead.

03 · The upright holder

A compact plate-holder structure with actual bottom grooves and edge-on objects, not a row of overlapping front-facing icons.

All items are seated in defined slots and retained by the holder's side rails. Increase face exposure to explore a more angled drawing.

04 · The exhibition shelf

Illustrated artworks hang against the backing. Sculptures, instruments, and parcels are anchored by their physical bases.

All images are symbolic drawings; they do not preview the contents of actual files. Hover a drawn object for its metadata description.
Scope: This is a browser source prototype with synthetic objects. Its geometry is calculated rather than hand-positioned. Single-scroll drops use constrained gravity steps and are not a full rigid-body dynamics engine. It does not modify your files, repository, or Quickshell installation. The next technical step is to translate the validated placement rules into QML policy and reuse the existing shelf widget directly.
