The music-box mechanism

Compare two approaches: a 12-minute revolution of the constellation-bearing upper drum, or a still upper drum with two illustrative constellation patterns placed left and right. The architectural trim and bearings remain fixed.

The broad arch is drawn in front of the cylinder and obscures some of the stars, so the disappearing constellations are a real layer-order effect rather than an artificial fade.

The arch uses a mathematically constant-width centerline offset, and the artwork aperture uses that same inner contour; there is no independently drawn curve to create a sliver. The original 282px drum is unchanged; only the short non-rotating collar meets the 328px dome.

Star arrangements are illustrative placeholders; verified astronomical mappings can replace them later.
