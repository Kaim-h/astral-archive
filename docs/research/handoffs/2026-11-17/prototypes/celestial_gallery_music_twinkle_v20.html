The idle FPS slider caps rendering only during idle searching. On click and Off click use every available browser animation frame rather than the idle cap; their smoothness still depends on the machine's rendering performance. Search speed is independent of FPS and can change mid-search. The scope now pauses between short, eased scans without rebuilding the scene during those pauses. The pause duration is adjustable, and neither the speed setting nor FPS cap affects the click animation. On click interrupts the search at its current pose and glides to 60° / 30°; Off click brings it back to the default rest pose. Both transitions use the same duration as the music fade. The lens twinkle fires only once the clicked pose is reached. A 10 FPS idle preview will look less fluid than a 60 FPS preview, but should require fewer dome redraws.
