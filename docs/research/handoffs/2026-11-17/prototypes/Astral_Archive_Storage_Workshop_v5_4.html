Interaction notes

Click a rack cell to inspect it. While the cell is open, its simulation remains active. Capacity checks prevent impossible initial loads and additions. Removing a scroll lets others settle downward. Inserting a scroll at a highlighted gap displaces its neighbors and lets the pile re-adjust itself.

Empty space is partitioned into actual free-space regions, drawn as their irregular shapes against scroll and wall boundaries. Click a region to lock it, then insert a new or held scroll. A smaller-than-scroll void can still be selected; neighbors glide aside before a scroll is inserted, if a valid solution exists. Fully packed cells reject additions safely.
