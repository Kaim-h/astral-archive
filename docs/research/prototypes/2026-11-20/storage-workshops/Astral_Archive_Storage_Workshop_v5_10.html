Interaction notes

Click a rack cell to inspect it. Click a scroll to select, or drag it at least 14 pixels: the scroll follows your pointer. Release it inside the cell to test a real collision-driven placement. Release outside to keep it in hand; drag it from the tray to try again. Only the active cell animates; settled piles sleep.

Insertion validates a physical trajectory to actual rest, then replays the same settings at fixed time steps.
