Interaction notes

Click a rack cell to inspect it. Click a scroll to select, or drag it at least 14 pixels: the same scroll follows your pointer. Release it inside the cell to push neighbors aside with live collisions. Release outside to keep it in hand; drag it again from the holding tray. Only the active cell simulates, and settled piles sleep.

Drag release uses the cursor position, with a small inward correction near walls rather than jumping to another pocket. A rejected placement keeps the scroll in hand; Undo returns it to its original place. Click-to-lock voids and the Insert / Drop from top buttons remain available, and Export/import preserves settled arrangements.
