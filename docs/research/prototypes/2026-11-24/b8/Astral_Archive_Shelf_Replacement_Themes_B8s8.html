B8s8 — Replacement theme case study

This study explores possible replacements for the weak cartographic family. It compares three new directions: Bestiary, Horological, and Elemental (a broader development of the earlier tidal idea). Each family has at least eight candidate emblems. The live preview keeps the preferred defaults of ornamented corners and linework glass reveal.

Family picker

4 visible at a time 1–4

Bestiary

library creatures · heraldic silhouettes

Bestiary draws from symbolic animals and winged forms. This family feels the most “storied” and could pair naturally with archives, myths and decorative woodwork.

Horological

antique instruments · measured time

Horological emphasizes dials, balances, pendulums and older clockwork symbolism rather than industrial steampunk clutter. It complements the Archive’s mechanical and contemplative sides.

Elemental

water, air, earth, fire

Elemental broadens the earlier tidal direction into the four elements. Water remains a strong branch, but the whole family can support atmospheric environmental themes, especially in the Map Room later.

Notes: This is a separate replacement-family case study rather than an integrated Shelf redesign. The goal is to decide which broad family deserves to replace or supplement cartographic ornamentation in the future. The contour-map / topographic background idea remains reserved for a dedicated Map Room study.
