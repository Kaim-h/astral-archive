Study notes

Preferred direction from B8s5: satin frosted glass, with visible hinges and handle retained. New question: if the backing field is theme-specific, its border and center embellishment should be theme-specific too.

Reverse frost The ornament is less frosted than the surrounding pane, so it reveals a little more of the contents without becoming fully transparent.
Thematic family Each family coordinates the border, corners and center motif rather than treating them as unrelated decorations.
Satin baseline Most door treatments here assume a satin-frosted door plane rather than a bare opening.
Comparative intent The goal is to identify parts worth carrying forward, not to lock in one final solution yet.

Families

Astral emphasizes circles, arcs and conjunctions. Cartographic uses compass geometry, calibrated ticks and directional symmetries. Botanical uses stems, leaves and scroll growth. Architectural keeps the language restrained through stepped inlay and measured geometry.

Possible next-direction synthesis

A plausible integrated direction would be: theme-specific backing families, a lighter corner-led border, and satin frosted cubby doors with a moderate reverse-frost emblem.
