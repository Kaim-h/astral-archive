Study limitations and controls

Canonical B7 SVG illustrations are embedded unchanged; rack masonry and cubby faces are adapted from B6/native source, not a live solver. Select a cubby to cycle Open, Veiled, Inventory and Closed in this visual study; production Browse enters and Arrange selects. Arrangement drag, support and size transactions are synthetic. No actual navigation, physics, filesystem actions, persistence, automated expansion solver or native rendering. Theme and profile are immediate visual comparisons. Width/height are provisional until Apply; Undo restores the last committed envelope. Backing engravings are non-obstructive and share the selected motif with exterior ornament. Door previews are visual materials over the same cubby aperture; no actual opening mechanics, collision or new container semantics are implemented. Upright selection preserves the same synthetic identity and reserved slot; no drag or disk operations. Module tops and wall-mounted boards support dependent objects; only compatible top surfaces are eligible.
