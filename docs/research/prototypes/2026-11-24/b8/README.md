## Owner decisions — accepted direction, November 24

- Hybrid architectural cabinetry and modular furniture, with a slight architectural-niche influence; Shelf must feel distinct from ordinary cubby.
- Shelf is a higher-order recursive cubby, not exclusively a cubby holder. It can directly contain racks, boards, nested cubbies, mounted artwork, plinths and other supported fixtures/objects.
- Freely positioned, physically supported rectangular modules, not compulsory rows.
- Configurable open and closed cubby previews.
- Decorative recessed panels in unused space.
- Predominantly stone with metal detailing; common design language.
- Ornament mainly additive, outside the usable rectangular interior. Avoid arches cutting into usable area; no nonrectangular module requirement.
- Module tops can act as shelves where sufficient overhead space exists. Suitable flat structural surfaces should be reusable support datums; exact eligibility rules remain to be designed.
- Hybrid envelope: manual dimensions remain authoritative, offer explicit expansion preview when needed. Ornament stays consistent during resizing.

## Existing contracts to preserve

Browse enters containers; Arrange selects, never enters on double click. Contextual creation and transient right-click menus, keyboard equivalents; explicit Apply/Cancel/Undo; independent grid visibility and snapping, same interval/local origin, no relocation when interval changes. Parent footprint and interior size independent. No silent resize/reflow of contents; block invalid shrink, optionally suggest conservative rearrangement. Fixed outer window, uniform fit; understated transitions (180–250 ms candidate). Universal Scroll Case. No disk operations in design studies. Preserve theme roles Evening/Twilight/Midnight. Room/Shelf character inherits common motif/material language with independent overrides; no finalized catalog.

## Candidates, not decisions

Initial alternatives: traditional bookcase (rigid), built-in cabinetry (coherent), modular stack (flexible), architectural niche (atmospheric but potentially subtractive). Selected hybrid incorporates cabinetry/modularity and a restrained niche character, not the earlier arch-shaped aperture. Candidate exterior treatments: rectangular pilasters, cornice, plinth, external crest/pediment, inlaid fascia; keep corners/crest unscaled, extend straight runs and repeat motifs as dimensions change. Recesses are decorative backing, not obstacles. Open cubby preview is a faithful miniature with navigation-level hit target; direct Shelf objects retain normal interactions, closed face same footprint. Candidate support policies: base, module top, ledge, appropriate wall attachment; move supporting module with dependents in preview, height change carries dependents if clearance permits, block unsupported shrink, removal requires resolution. No structural engineering simulation. Generalizing tops to ordinary cubbies is proposed, not separately approved implementation scope.

## B8s3 — visual continuity and thematic identity (candidate, 2026-11-25)

Owner agreed the backing should convey thematic identity without reading as a door; exact motifs remain experimental. [B8s3 standalone HTML](Astral_Archive_Shelf_Study_B8s3.html) is an independently authored revision of the exact public B8s2 baseline (blob 7b9fe4caf2f41dad0adf096fdaac9f2f457fe9b3). It preserves the existing rectangular envelope, cubby previews, fixed sample arrangement, theme palette, scroll depiction and reversible size edits. It adds four non-obstructive motif options (astral, cartographic, botanical, plain); the backing uses engravings without a panel boundary. Crest and backing share the motif; the base frame remains consistent. The motif selector is a study comparison, not a settled inheritance or customization UI.

Uprights use B7 U3-derived thickness-dependent slot widths (thickness*scale+12, horizontal fit), rail, cheeks, separators, fascia dots and a supported adjacent cradle. Original edge and face art are distinct. Presenting one of four synthetic entries removes its edge from the holder, reserves its slot, and displays its face; selecting another returns the previous entry. The reserved slot or return control restores the piece. No file transfer or drag is simulated. Nested cubby previews are noninteractive at file level. Compact file plaques are icon/engraving marks rather than persistent filename text; anchored, viewport-clamped metadata is available on hover/focus/click and dismissible with Escape. File details are synthetic. Filenames on direct file representations are not visible at rest; named navigation containers retain their plaques. The sample initially presents a ledger to demonstrate the contract. The decorative ornaments and exact fixture scales are candidates, not accepted production requirements.

## B8s5 — door materials and ornamental corner case study (candidate, 2026-11-25)

Owner requested stronger frost, with physical cubby doors as the visual premise, and richer alternatives to B8s4's simple corner marks. Stronger blur (4.5 and 7.2 units on the existing miniature coordinate system), translucent surface washes, restrained hinges, frames and pulls are illustrative treatments only. No actual door motion, swing clearance, locking, ownership or additional storage mode is implemented. Opaque choices omit the underlying content depiction. Three sample interiors, all existing themes/motifs, independent live Shelf selection and original preview/reset/metadata/presentation/return/resize behaviors remain available.

Corner field comparison: no border, simple corners, stepped inlay, diamond finials, foliate scrollwork, celestial tracery, carved fan brackets and motif-matched corners. The latter maps to a fixed candidate grammar by motif (astral to celestial tracery, cartographic to diamond, botanical to foliate, plain to stepped). Thus it intentionally reproduces an existing corner geometry rather than claiming a distinct shape for every theme. Corner detail has increased from 18-unit simple marks to a bounded 37-unit ornamental footprint, with no placement impact. Motif changes refresh both door and corner galleries. No candidate is selected as the final implementation.
