### Owner review — theme-coordinated application book colours

The owner approved moving beyond the dedicated Shelf customization studies and added a development requirement: application books must have three adaptive, theme-coordinated colour variants for Twilight, Evening and Midnight. Interpret this as a stable per-application colour identity with a coordinated variant for each desktop theme, rather than the current single theme-independent AppMatching.bindingColor swatch or random reassignment on theme change. Coordinate cover, inscription legibility, ornament and existing running/focused state indicators with the applicable palette. The exact swatches, mapping algorithm, and whether further user overrides are offered remain design/implementation questions; no new colour catalogue is approved by this note. Preserve independent fixed-size book representations, application identities and board support. S18r4 is a bounded placement/interaction reference, not the final book colour or bookmark artwork specification. Bookmark motion remains a separate deferred design branch. No prototype or native source changes. Proceed to Map Room discussion; earlier B9 furniture and tentative B10 discoveries deferrals remain.
